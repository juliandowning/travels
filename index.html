When to go

Australia & New Zealand
Reversed seasons — summer is Dec–Feb. Australia: dry north Apr–Oct; south Sep–Nov or Mar–May. NZ: Dec–Mar.
India, Sri Lanka & Maldives
Dry season Oct/Nov to Mar/Apr. South and west coast: Dec–Apr.
South Africa
Safari: dry winter May–Oct. Garden Route: Feb–Apr for warm, dry weather.
Holland / Amsterdam
Peak tulip bloom mid-April to early May — Keukenhof open late March to mid-May. In 2029 to give Japan April 2028.
Switzerland / Norway
June–August for mountain hiking and fjords.
UK
May–Sep warmest and driest. May/June preferred; Sep for Scotland (fewer midges). April notably dry for Snowdon.

Season words

Early / late
First or second half of a month or season. Late often means weather at its best.
Peak / best
The destination at its absolute best — usually busiest and most expensive.
Shoulder
Just before or after peak: very good conditions, fewer people, better prices.
Dry starts / ends
Start or end of the dry season — key for tropical trips and safari.
