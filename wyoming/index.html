🍆 October in Yellowstone — Why This Timing Is Perfect
Summer crowds are gone. Aspens are turning gold. Grizzlies are in hyperphagia — gorging 20,000 calories a day before hibernation, which means they’re highly active and visible at all hours. The elk rut is at its peak — bulls bugling across valleys at dawn. Wolves are active and bold. This is genuinely one of the finest wildlife-watching windows on the planet.
🌳 Leg I — Wyoming
Days 1–3 · Tue 12 – Thu 14 October

Jackson Hole

Fly direct into Jackson Hole Airport (JAC) — one of the most dramatic airport approaches in America, landing between the Tetons and the National Elk Refuge. Three nights here: Grand Teton National Park, the Snake River, and one of the best small towns in the American West.

Day 1
Arrival

Fly London → Jackson Hole (JAC) via connecting flight (Denver or Salt Lake City are typical hubs). Collect your 4WD SUV — essential for the roads ahead. Check in and get your bearings on the Town Square.

🚗 Hire a 4WD SUV
A capable 4WD is essential throughout. Book well in advance for October — peak wildlife season brings serious demand. Drop off in Austin on Day 16.
Day 2
Grand Teton
⛰
Grand Teton National Park
The Teton Range rises 7,000 feet straight from the valley floor with no foothills — one of the most dramatic mountain fronts in North America. Drive the Teton Park Road at dawn for moose, bison, and the light on the peaks. Stop at Oxbow Bend for mirror reflections.
DAWN LIGHTMOOSE & BISON
🚣
Snake River Float Trip
A guided float down the Snake River with the Tetons behind you. Ospreys, bald eagles, otters and moose along the banks. October is a beautiful time — golden cottonwoods lining the river, far fewer people than summer.

Days 4–7 · Fri 15 – Mon 18 October

Yellowstone
National Park

Four nights in the world’s first national park. The Lamar Valley at dawn for bears, wolves and bison. Geysers, hot springs, and 10,000 geothermal features. This is the wildlife heartland of North America — and October is when it truly comes alive.

🐾 Bear Watching Strategy
Lamar Valley is your priority — arrive before dawn and stay until 10am, then return at dusk. October grizzlies are highly visible as they feed intensively before denning. Bring binoculars and a spotting scope if you have one. The valley also has the highest wolf population in the lower 48 — the Lamar Canyon pack is frequently visible. Hayden Valley is a secondary option for bears and bison.
Day 4
Arrival

Drive 100km north from Jackson through the John D. Rockefeller Parkway. Enter Yellowstone via the South Entrance. Stop at West Thumb Geyser Basin — hot springs right on the edge of Yellowstone Lake, steam rising from the water. Check in and orientate.

Day 5
Lamar Valley
🐾
Lamar Valley — The Serengeti of North America
Up before dawn for the drive to Lamar Valley — the greatest wildlife spectacle in America. In October: grizzly bears in hyperphagia, bison herds, elk bugling in the rut, and wolves if you’re lucky (Lamar Canyon pack). Bring a long lens. The light at sunrise over the valley is extraordinary. Stay all morning, return at dusk.
GRIZZLY BEARSWOLVESELK RUTDAWN START
Day 6
Geysers
💧
Old Faithful & Upper Geyser Basin
The world’s most famous geyser erupts every 60–90 minutes to 55 metres. Walk the Upper Geyser Basin boardwalk — the greatest concentration of geysers on Earth.
ERUPTS EVERY 90 MINS
🌞
Grand Prismatic Spring
Yellowstone’s largest hot spring — 90 metres across, vivid rings of orange, yellow and green from heat-loving bacteria around a deep blue centre. Best viewed from the overlook trail above. Unmissable.
OVERLOOK TRAILLARGEST HOT SPRING

Hayden Valley
The other great wildlife corridor — bison herds, grizzlies fishing for cutthroat trout, coyotes, trumpeter swans. A dawn drive here is never wasted.
BISON HERDSGRIZZLIES

The Yellowstone River drops 94 metres over the Lower Falls into a canyon of vivid yellow and orange rock. Views from Artist Point are genuinely jaw-dropping.

Days 8–9 · Tue 19 – Wed 20 October

Cody, Wyoming

Named after Buffalo Bill Cody, this is the most authentically Western town in America. Drive east through the stunning Wapiti Valley out of Yellowstone — one of the most scenic mountain drives in Wyoming. Two nights in cowboy country.

Drive
~1h 30m

The drive east from Yellowstone’s East Entrance through the Wapiti Valley along the North Fork of the Shoshone River is magnificent — hoodoos, canyon walls, and the last wild wolves outside the park territory. Keep your eyes open.

Cody
🏰
Buffalo Bill Center of the West
Five world-class museums in one complex: the Buffalo Bill Museum, Whitney Western Art Museum, Plains Indian Museum, Natural History Museum, and Firearms Museum. Genuinely one of the great museums of the American West. Allow a full morning.
5 MUSEUMSFULL MORNING
🤠
Rodeo & Western Culture
The Cody Nite Rodeo runs June–August so just misses October, but Cody’s rodeo spirit never really stops. Check local listings for the NRA Finals Rodeo in Billings (usually mid-October) — it’s 3 hours north but worth it if the dates align. The Irma Hotel Bar, built by Buffalo Bill himself in 1902, is a must regardless.
CHECK NRA FINALS DATESIRMA HOTEL 1902
🎪 The NRA Finals Rodeo — Billings, MT
The National Rodeo Association Finals typically runs mid-October in Billings, Montana — about 3 hours north of Cody. If the dates land right for your trip (check montanaprorodeo.org as October 2027 approaches), this is genuine championship rodeo: bull riding, barrel racing, team roping. Worth building the Montana leg around it.
Stay
The Classic
The Irma Hotel
12th Street, Cody
Built by Buffalo Bill Cody in 1902 and named after his daughter. A genuine piece of American West history — the cherrywood bar was a gift from Queen Victoria. This is where you stay in Cody.
From ~$140/night
⛰ Leg II — Montana
Days 10–11 · Thu 21 – Fri 22 October

Bozeman, Montana

Drive 3 hours north from Cody into the Big Sky State. Bozeman sits in the Gallatin Valley, surrounded by mountains on three sides, with the Bridger Range to the north and the Gallatin Canyon to the south. Lively university town, excellent food, genuine ranch country.

Drive
~3h

The drive north from Cody through the Bighorn Basin and into the Gallatin Valley is big, open, Montana at its most itself. Stop in Red Lodge for lunch — a beautiful Victorian mining town at the foot of the Beartooth Range.

Bozeman
🏔
Gallatin Canyon Drive
Drive south on Highway 191 through the Gallatin Canyon — the river that runs alongside in a narrow gorge, the mountains pressing in on both sides. A River Runs Through It country. Stop at Ousel Falls for a short hike.
A RIVER RUNS THROUGH ITOUSEL FALLS
🏰
Museum of the Rockies
One of the finest natural history museums in the US, with the world’s largest collection of dinosaur fossils. The T-Rex specimens alone are worth the visit. Part of Montana State University.
WORLD’S BEST DINO FOSSILS
🍴 Dinner: Bozeman’s Food Scene
MAP Brewing Co for craft beer and views of the Bridger Range. Blackbird Kitchen for a proper dinner — local beef, foraged mushrooms, excellent wine list. Bozeman punches well above its size for restaurants.
Stay
The Best Address
The Kimpton Armory Hotel
Main Street, Bozeman
The grandest hotel in Bozeman — in a beautifully restored 1941 National Guard Armory. Soaring ceilings, exposed brick, excellent bar. Central location, walking distance to everything.
From ~$220/night
⭐ Leg III — Texas
Day 12 · Sat 23 October

Austin Arrives

Fly Bozeman direct to Austin — connections via Salt Lake City or Denver, typically 3.5–4 hours total. Arrive by afternoon, one night in the city before driving to the wedding in the morning. The bats, 6th Street, The Driskill.

Fly
✈ Bozeman (BZN) → Austin (AUS)
No direct flight but excellent one-stop connections — Delta via Salt Lake City or United via Denver, typically 3.5–4 hours total. Arrive mid-afternoon. Book ahead: October is busy in both cities.
Dusk
🐸
Congress Avenue Bridge Bat Colony
1.5 million Mexican free-tailed bats emerge at dusk in a continuous dark ribbon. Late October is the very end of the season before they migrate — check batcon.org for 2027 emergence dates. Arrive 20 minutes early. Free, unmissable.
1.5 MILLION BATSFREE & UNMISSABLE
Evening

6th Street for a quick wander and live music. Dinner close to the hotel — keep it easy. Early start in the morning for the drive to Marble Falls.

The Retreat at Balcones Springs · Marble Falls, Texas · 27–28 October 2027
Days 13–14 · Sun 26 – Mon 27 October

The Wedding

Fly Denver → Austin (2h 40m), collect your car, and drive 90 minutes northwest to the Texas Hill Country. The Retreat at Balcones Springs: 250 acres of live oak and juniper, a private lake, and nine wedding venues on a single property. This is what it was all heading towards.

The Venue
🏠
The Retreat at Balcones Springs
104 Balcones Springs Drive, Marble Falls TX — 45 minutes north of Austin, near Marble Falls in the Texas Hill Country. 250 acres of live oak and juniper land. Private lake (Lake Ted). Nine wedding venues on site. Luxury cabin-style lodging, full-service gourmet catering, pool, horseback riding, zip line, bonfires. Family-run since 1993. A complete weekend on a single property.
250 ACRESPRIVATE LAKELUXURY CABINS ON SITE
🌵 Texas Hill Country in October
Late October in the Hill Country is perfect — temperatures around 20–25°C, dry, with stunning golden light. The live oaks turn copper and the juniper is vivid green. Some of the most beautiful landscape in Texas.
Day 15 · Tuesday 28 October

Austin — The Last Night

Wedding done. Drive 90 minutes northeast from Marble Falls back into Austin. One final night in the city before flying home tomorrow. Franklin for lunch if you can manage it, bats at dusk, one last whiskey at The Driskill Bar.

Dusk
🐸
Congress Avenue Bridge Bat Colony
1.5 million Mexican free-tailed bats emerge from under the bridge at dusk in a continuous dark ribbon for 20–30 minutes. Late October is the very end of the season before they migrate south — check the Bat Conservation International site for 2027 emergence dates. Get there 20 minutes early. Completely free, completely unmissable.
